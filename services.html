Services

Quality services from assessment to therapy, under one roof.

Afterschool support for students in inclusive schools, small groups for homeschoolers and NIOS students, and early intervention for young children.

Who we support

Children with dyslexia, specific learning difficulties, ADHD, autism, developmental delays, emotional and behavioural challenges, and learning gaps.

How sessions run

In-centre, in Kumara Park. Remedial sessions are one-on-one; groups are 4 to 6. Online only for families outside Bengaluru.

Fees

Affordable and transparent. Packages are built around your family’s needs, so we don’t publish a standard price. Talk to us.

Assessments and diagnostics

Children of all school ages, with or without a diagnosis

Psycho-educational, occupational therapy and special education assessments that help us understand a child and set the right goals.

Format: In-centre, by appointment

  • •Psycho-educational assessment
  • •Occupational therapy assessment
  • •Special education assessment
  • •Written report shared with the family

Remedial education

Students with dyslexia, specific learning difficulties, ADHD and learning gaps

One-on-one and small-group interventions that build the skills behind school work: reading, comprehension, writing, spelling and executive functioning.

Format: One-on-one or groups of 4 to 6; minimum one term (3 months)

  • •Reading, phonics and literacy interventions
  • •Numeracy remediation
  • •Writing and comprehension support
  • •Executive functioning and study skills
  • •Assistive technology support

Clinical and therapeutic sessions

Children needing speech, occupational, behavioural or emotional support

Speech and language therapy, occupational therapy, behavioural support, counselling and play, art and music therapy, integrated with education.

Format: In-centre, delivered with MoU-backed expert partners

  • •Speech and language therapy, including AAC
  • •Occupational therapy and sensory integration
  • •Positive behaviour support and emotional regulation
  • •Counselling, play, art and music therapy
  • •Cognitive training

Early intervention

Young children in the early years

Proven programs for young students that help reduce wiring difficulties early, when support has the greatest effect.

Format: In-centre, individual and small group

  • •Early identification
  • •Developmental support
  • •Play-based, activity-based learning
  • •Parent guidance

Parent coaching and counselling

Parents and families

Experienced parent coaches help you interpret professional inputs, set up home support and navigate milestones, so the special need is not the only focus of family life.

Format: Alongside your child's sessions

  • •Intake that values parental intuition
  • •Parent coaching and review meetings
  • •Situational counselling: school choices, behaviour, adolescence
  • •Parent pod at the centre
  • •Parent support groups (planned)

Homeschool and NIOS support

Homeschoolers and NIOS students

Small-group support working towards specific skills and subjects, plus bridge courses and exam preparation.

Format: Small groups, in-centre

  • •Subject and skill groups
  • •Bridge courses
  • •NIOS course support
  • •Exam preparation support
