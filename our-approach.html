Our approach

What happens when you come to C.A.N

Plain language, no surprises. Here is the journey for you and for your child, stage by stage.

You don’t need a diagnosis to start. The first conversation is about your child’s strengths, needs and aspirations, and about your intuition as a parent, which we value as much as any report. From there we build a team, a plan and a rhythm of support that we review and adapt together.

  1. 1

    Understand

    Discover the child behind the challenge

    We listen, observe, assess, and learn about the child's strengths, needs, and aspirations.

    For the parent

    An intake conversation that values your intuition as much as professional inputs.

    For the child

    Low-pressure sessions to get to know the centre and the people in it.

    Typical timeline

    First 2 to 3 weeks

  2. 2

    Connect

    Bring the right people together

    We bring together remedial education, therapeutic interventions, psychological support, and inclusive learning practices to create a connected support system for children and their families.

    For the parent

    One team around your child: special educators, therapists, counsellors and a parent coach.

    For the child

    Familiar faces who talk to each other, so nothing has to be explained twice.

    Typical timeline

    Weeks 2 to 4

  3. 3

    Plan

    Create a pathway that fits

    We establish meaningful, individualised goals and identify strategies that work for the child.

    For the parent

    An Individualized Education Plan (IEP) shared with you, with goals you helped shape.

    For the child

    Goals that make sense to them, in words they understand.

    Typical timeline

    By the end of month 1

  4. 4

    Support

    Build skills. Nurture confidence.

    We provide targeted remedial, therapeutic, and developmental support through engaging, child-centred practices and internationally validated programs.

    For the parent

    Parent coaching alongside sessions, and a parent pod to work or unwind while your child is with us.

    For the child

    One-on-one remedial sessions and small groups of 4 to 6, at a pace that fits.

    Typical timeline

    Ongoing, in terms of 3 months

  5. 5

    Review

    Reflect, adapt, grow

    We monitor progress, listen to feedback, and refine our approach as the child moves ahead in their life journey.

    For the parent

    Monthly internal reviews across every professional, and a written progress report every term.

    For the child

    Celebrating what's working and adjusting what isn't.

    Typical timeline

    Monthly internal, termly with parents

  6. 6

    Empower

    Prepare for a world of possibilities

    We build self-advocacy and the confidence to participate more independently in everyday life, working towards making ourselves redundant.

    For the parent

    Planning for school transitions, accommodations and what comes next.

    For the child

    The skills and confidence to fly on their own strengthened wings.

    Typical timeline

    As skills are mastered
