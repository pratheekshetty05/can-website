Getting started

Do we need a diagnosis to approach you?+

No. Please reach out and we can help regardless of whether your child has been diagnosed. We also have in-house diagnostic services if that is required.

How much does it cost?+

We are affordable and transparent. The package you get is based on your family's needs, so we can't put a standard package on the website. Do drop in and help us help you.

What are your timings?+

We work Monday to Friday, 10 am to 6:30 pm.

Where are you located?+

In Kumarapark, Bengaluru. Our centre is set up with the resources and physical facilities children need; we don't do home visits.

Do you have online services?+

Only for families who don't live in Bengaluru. We work offline with students for maximum impact.

Is this a school or a clinic?+

Neither. It is a home away from home for students: a space where they feel understood and safe while learning critical skills. We want our kids to walk in with a smile most days, even when the skills are challenging.

Families

Who is CAN for?+

CAN is primarily for families who support children with dyslexia, specific learning difficulties, ADHD and autism. We also work with teachers and schools in creating inclusive and informed educational eco-systems.

My child is struggling at school. Can you help?+

Yes. We will help you understand the struggles further and see what kind of support a child needs. Sometimes simple, specific home support is the answer. In other cases we might need a customised program. In short, we can help, but the help might come in different forms.

What's your student to teacher ratio?+

Remedial sessions are one-on-one. Group sessions are small, around 4 to 6 students. Depending on the need, we might also run one-off community sessions with larger groups.

How long before we see progress?+

It is hard to say because there are many variables. In our experience, when all required services are provided consistently and home support is constant, students show incremental shifts on several fronts in 2 to 3 terms (a term is 3 months).

How do you track progress?+

After detailed assessments we create Individualized Education Plans and share them with the family. Every professional working with a child reports progress at a monthly internal meeting, and once a term a progress report is shared with parents.

Can I join for one month?+

No. The minimum enrolment is one term, which is 3 months.

Will my child's grades improve?+

We build skills that help academic performance: reading, writing, spelling, oral expression, perception and more. Over time children benefit, even though we don't work towards increasing grades directly. We don't complete school portions for any subject.

How long does therapy take? Will my child need it forever?+

It depends on the child and the difficulty. Nothing is forever: children grow, learn, cope and thrive. Consistent therapy matters for results. Plans change as a child masters skills, and we work towards making ourselves redundant in a child's life. They should fly because we have strengthened their wings.

What is your policy on assessments?+

In primary years we prefer to work with a child for a few months before asking for an assessment. In middle years we might ask you to work with our assessment professionals so your child can access accommodations at school, as is their right.

What is your cancellation policy?+

Since we block a professional's time one-on-one, we need 48 hours' notice for cancellation. We understand things crop up and can be flexible case by case.

Are your programs evidence-based?+

Yes. Neuroscience is our friend and we stay up to date with credible research. All our programs are benchmarked to international standards and adapted to the Indian context.

What if my child needs something you can't provide?+

We try to be comprehensive, but we can't be everything to everyone. If your child needs something more or different, we will refer you to professionals. After that, it is your choice.

What parent support do you provide?+

The centre is also for parents. We coach parents on their child's needs, counsel on setting up home support, and provide situational counselling when milestones arise (adolescence, specific behaviours). Solutions-oriented parent support groups are planned.

What is expected of me as a parent?+

Consistent attendance, home support as discussed with CAN, attending parent coaching and review meetings, and communicating honestly with CAN about your child's strengths, needs and difficulties on a continual basis.

Do you provide transport?+

No, we do not provide transport.

I'm afraid of what a diagnosis might mean.+

A diagnosis is a starting point for supporting a child, not a destination set in stone. It brings relief in seeing the cause of underlying difficulties and helps all of us support the child better. All information about your child stays confidential.

Safety and privacy

How safe is your centre?+

We have child-proofed the centre for all age groups, with CCTV (without audio) in sessions. We have child safeguarding policies and intensive training to keep children safe physically, mentally and emotionally at all times.

Will my child's images be shared?+

We will not share a child's face on the website, even if a parent consents. With parental consent we might share pictures of a child's work or hands on social media. Without consent, we publish nothing.

How is my child's data protected?+

Child information is confidential, accessed on a need-to-know basis. Social media uses changed names and non-identifying information. We follow all of the country's laws and go beyond them to protect our students.

Can I take photos or videos of sessions?+

Therapists invite parents to sessions to show techniques. In those spaces you can photograph or record only your child, for home follow-up. These are not meant for public posting.

Schools and professionals

How can a school refer a child?+

Share our website with the child's parents and let them make an appointment. Feel free to email us to let us know you have referred the child.

Will you tell us what's happening with a child we referred?+

By law we can only communicate with the parents. If parents consent, we would love to partner with schools and teachers. We will not share reports or information with a school without written parental consent.

Do you work with schools directly?+

Yes. We train all teachers to teach all kinds of students, help schools set up SEN systems, support accommodations filing with boards of education, train SEN professionals, and mentor middle management.

Are you hiring? Can I intern?+

Check our careers page or email can@wadhwafoundation.com. Students in psychology, special education, occupational therapy, speech language pathology, social work or related fields are welcome to apply. Not from these fields? Email us anyway.

Are you registered and legally compliant?+

Yes, we are.
